# Designer_Univers Brand Identity Harness

## When to Use

Use for draft brand strategy, naming, slogan/tone, portfolio framing, or visual direction work where Hyeon's protected identity/philosophy material may be a private lens.

## Procedure

1. Route with `designer-univers-expert-router` and start L1 unless public benchmark research or full visual direction is requested.
2. Treat `_Identity`, `_Philosophy`, `About Me`, `Storytelling_First`, and `Background_Before_Design` as read-only lenses.
3. Draft outputs under System/Runs path contracts only; do not publish or promote copy automatically.
4. Require `identity-lens-reviewer` whenever protected-source fit matters.
5. Require `safety-reviewer` before promotion candidates or public-facing use.

## Path Contract

- strategy: `workspace/01_brand_strategy.md`
- naming: `workspace/02_naming_candidates.md`
- verbal: `workspace/03_verbal_identity.md`
- visual: `workspace/04_visual_identity.md`
- identity review: `review/05_identity_lens_review.md`
- safety review: `review/06_safety_review.md`
- final draft: `outputs/07_brand_identity_draft.md`

## Verification

- No protected source text is copied into public-facing draft without explicit approval.
- Trademark/domain claims are marked user-verification-needed.
- Visual suggestions remain text directions unless a separate image generation task is approved.
